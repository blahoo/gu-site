# EurekaHACKS: Sponsorship Lead

> Sponsorship lead for EurekaHACKS, a student-run high school hackathon at Abbey Park High School in Oakville: I raised nearly $11,000 for the 2025 event from local businesses and tech organizations.

EurekaHACKS is a 12-hour hackathon run by high school students at Abbey Park High School in Oakville. After co-founding [Apocalypse](/sidequests/apoc) the year before, I joined the organizing team for the 2025 event as sponsorship lead.

The part I cared about most is who it's for: it's built to welcome first-time coders as much as experienced hackers.

## Sponsorship

I raised nearly $11,000 in funding from local businesses and tech organizations. That work was featured in the Halton News for building ties between local businesses, the Halton District School Board and youth STEM in the area.

The 2025 sponsor list included DoraHacks, Jane Street, the Perimeter Institute, CodeCrafters, Mathnasium Oakville and more than 15 others.

## Logistics

Beyond sponsorship, I supported logistics: sourcing food vendors, managing the event budget, and finding cost-effective options for decorations.
